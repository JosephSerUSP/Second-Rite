PE Day 1 · basement rat on the real-time battle scheduler

Captured 2026-10-06 with native LÖVE preview-scene on the staged pe-day1 Project. Each frame is driven by controller input on the real scene. Every hit, cost, heal, reward and outcome is resolved by production Battle:executeTurn (#1424). Amber notes mark capture-only setups such as forced rat choices or preset HP. These are automated captures, not owner playtest acceptance.

Play: node studio/editor/project-cli.js play projects/pe-day1. Arrows move; Enter aims and fires at full AT; X opens the command menu; Esc cancels a menu or leaves.
