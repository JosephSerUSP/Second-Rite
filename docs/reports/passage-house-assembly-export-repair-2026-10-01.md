## Render policy and controls

Draft and export atlases stay 1024 square. Shared Cycles export quality is 64 samples, neutral exposure, four total bounces and fast bundled OIDN. Draft/lookdev use 64 samples; review photographs retain a 128-sample option. OIDN filters each UV chart separately, pads it with only its own colors, preserves alpha/gutters and retains small detail charts. It is beauty-only filtering: material detail still needs visual inspection. Explicit raw and unsnapped controls are `--atlas-denoise none` and `--no-uv-texel-align`.

A matched-layout study compared repaired revision 13 at 64 raw, 64 denoised and 128 raw samples. RGB mean squared byte error against the noisy 128-sample control fell from 5.34877 to 4.66305 (12.82%); mean absolute error fell from 0.85938 to 0.82206. This is a reference-relative measurement, not a ground-truth quality score. The study filtered after PNG reload; the production adapter filters the linear bake before saving. Final aligned UVs cannot be compared with that control atlas pixel for pixel.

Nearest-centre rounding proved unsafe for densely packed UVs: it could close gutters and cause separate charts to share texels. The final adapter moves UV boundaries inward to texel centres and retains the original chart if it would leave its original bounds, collapse/flip a triangle or introduce shared texels. Bake dilation remains. Real bundled-OIDN controls verify chart isolation, unchanged alpha, preserved small detail and a narrow-gutter negative control.
