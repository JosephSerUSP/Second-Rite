Acceptance boundary and next improvements

This is a playable 3D prototype. Character articulation, authored attack/recoil animation, navigation around auditorium furniture, stronger Carnegie Hall architectural fidelity and encounter sound remain unfinished. Movement is bounded to the aisle; it does not use mesh collision. The rat uses a visible target-area attack, not a finished contact animation.

Behavior tests cover held/released input, diagonal speed, frame partitions, Euclidean attack range, hit/evade, victory/defeat and replay. Production G1/G2/G3/G4 and unit checks passed. Absolute G5/G6 remain red: references were preserved, and no claim of canonical visual acceptance is made. Native Effekseer coverage and owner playtest remain open.
