# Luna critic — gpt-5.6-luna

**Status:** completed after staged-project validation. Read only after owner
rating.

## Central thesis

Glasswake is a compact pressure-route about returning changed: town preparation,
Bellroot risk, Chasm re-entry, Rusted Choir confrontation, then a black-glass
choice make prior resource decisions feel carried forward.

## Strongest ideas

1. Clear escalation from town to Bellroot to Chasm to Rusted Choir.
2. Permadeath and shared MP reinforce the premise of who returns.
3. The black-glass ledger and explicit completion card create a memorable,
   auditable end to a noncanonical route.

## Weaknesses / pacing risks

The critic predicts risk clustering before Rusted Choir, an ending that may be
too textual if it does not reflect expedition history, and too many named
anchors for the compressed duration. Its likely pacing collapse is the segment
after Chasm pressure and before the Dragon. Likely owner favorite: the ledger
ending. Likely owner complaint: it may not visibly remember enough of the run.

## Canon distinction

It identifies shared expedition MP, creature permadeath, Charges/Overcast, and
meaningful town return as specifically Second Gate; ordinary recruit/trap/boss
furniture as generic. It recommends stealing endings that audit a run and
leaving the blue-chalk/black-glass presentation and short format experimental.
