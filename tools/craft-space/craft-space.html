Hue plane — Red / Green / Blue

Angle is elemental direction, radius is saturation, centre is non-elemental. Dots are the items this discipline can produce, drawn in their own derived colour; the glow is where its crafters' ideations land.

Value axis — Black ↔ White  (y = intensity)

The reach line marks where the selected crafter's grasp fades. Items above it are not forbidden, only distant — scatter occasionally carries a craft up there, which is the whole of the "barely reached it" effect. No anomaly, no crit.
