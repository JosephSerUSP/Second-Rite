# OpenRouter critic — z-ai/glm-5.2:free

**Status:** completed after staged-project validation. Read only after owner
rating.

## Central thesis

The critic finds that shared MP, permanent creatures, and town return survive
compression, but warns that the systems may be tuned for longer expeditions.

## Strongest ideas

1. Chasm as a return-pressure fulcrum.
2. The borrowed-room concept before the Rusted Choir confrontation.
3. The terminal black-glass choice.

## Weaknesses / pacing risks

It flags the lack of bespoke art as a risk to the visibility of the blue-chalk
premise, Bellroot frontloading too much teaching, and the explicit completion
card as potentially breaking immersion. It predicts the Chasm → Rusted Choir
transition as the likely pacing collapse and recommends a decompression beat.

## Canon distinction

It would steal a mid-expedition merchant at an MP inflection point, borrowed
territory boss concepts, and terminal expedition choices. It recommends keeping
the short-form compression, blue-chalk convention, and experimental completion
card out of canon by default.
