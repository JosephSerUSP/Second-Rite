By framing the physical toll of descent as "ash" by St. Maria and a "receipt" by the Labyrinth, the campaign interrogates whether the suffering endured in the dungeon is a spiritual trial or a mere economic exchange. The final binary—carrying the ember back versus breaking it over the contract—is not just a narrative fork; it is the player’s ultimate verdict on whether they accept the Labyrinth’s transactional reality or reject it in favor of St. Maria’s spiritual framing. It is a tight, thematic argument disguised as a 45-minute dungeon crawl.
