# HOW TO PLAY

Double-click `PLAY_CAMPAIGN.cmd`, choose **New Game**, and play until the
`GLASSWAKE COMPLETE` card appears. No debug tools or JSON edits are needed.

# EXPECTED LENGTH

About 45–75 minutes for a first run. The intended critical route is St. Maria
→ Bellroot Depths → Chasm Crossing → Rusted Choir → the Glasswake ending.
Returning to town between expeditions is part of the rhythm, not a failure.

# KNOWN ROUGH EDGES

This is an intentionally noncanonical machine-authored experiment built from
the current Second Gate Project. It reuses the live combat, creature, ritual,
town, and dungeon primitives, so some existing incidental text still belongs
to the source Project. The critical path and its opening/ending are specific to
Glasswake. It has not received a bespoke art pass.

## Premise

Each morning St. Maria wakes with blue-chalk names on its palms. Descend with
Saban, discover why the Labyrinth is keeping a ledger, and decide what a town
owes its creatures when it calls them companions.

## Route hints without solutions

- Speak to St. Maria’s regulars before and after a descent; their short scenes
  are the social half of the experiment.
- The Bellroot Depths teach recruitment, traps, and the price of carrying a
  second creature. Their blue chalk is a recurring clue.
- Chasm Crossing is a pressure-and-return chapter. Town Portal is a real
  retreat tool; use it before shared MP turns a good expedition into a loss.
- Rusted Choir contains the final threshold. Prepare, then confront the Red
  Dragon Guardian to conclude the campaign.

## Campaign-specific authorship

Glasswake is an experimental interpretation, not Second Gate canon. Its blue
chalk premise, compression, final interpretation of the ledger, and two ending
variants are campaign-specific authorship. Existing systems and base content
remain authored by the current Project.
